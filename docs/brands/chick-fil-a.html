CSW Analysis

Chick-fil-A is not competing in the same economic universe as the rest of the category. A $9.2M average unit volume on a six-day operating week means a single Chick-fil-A generates roughly what four Popeyes generate, and it does it with the industry's most sought-after operator pipeline. The interesting question is no longer whether Chick-fil-A wins the U.S. chicken sandwich war — it is what happens when unit volumes plateau. Systemwide sales growth of 5.2% in 2025 was driven substantially by the 179-unit build, and Restaurant Business has flagged that AUVs are pressing against a practical throughput ceiling. That is precisely why the $1B international push and the licensed-venue channel matter: they are the two remaining growth vectors that do not require squeezing more cars through the same drive-thru.

Chick-fil-A in the news

2026-07-13Real Estate

New-construction Chick-fil-A ground lease trades at a 4.15% cap — a Tennessee record6

What it means for the Chicken Wars: $2.77M for a 6,008 SF relocation store on 1.74 acres in Hixson, on a 15-year corporate absolute-NNN ground lease with 10% bumps. The lowest cap rate ever recorded for a single-tenant Chick-fil-A drive-thru in Tennessee, per CoStar — and it was achieved on a shorter-than-standard term. Chick-fil-A ground leases are now priced closer to bonds than to restaurants.

2026Brands

Chick-fil-A systemwide sales approach $24 billion on a $9.16M average unit volume7

What it means for the Chicken Wars: $23.9B, +5.2%, on 2,863 outlets plus 435 licensed venues. Six operating days a week. The growth came from 179 new restaurants rather than same-store volume, which is why the $1B international commitment and the licensed channel are now the strategic priorities — the domestic drive-thru is running out of hours in the day.
