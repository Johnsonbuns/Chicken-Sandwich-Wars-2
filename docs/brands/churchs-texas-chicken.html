CSW Analysis

Church's spent a decade shrinking; in 2025 it stopped. The U.S. system added 12 net units to 885, sales rose to $862M and AUV climbed to $1.138M — the third consecutive year of AUV improvement off a ~$1.0M base. That is a genuine stabilization, but it should be read for what it is: Church's is a value brand with roughly one-eighth the unit volume of Chick-fil-A, operating in trade areas where the consumer is most exposed to price. The international pipeline (600 units in China, 900 across Europe and beyond) is where the unit growth story actually lives.
