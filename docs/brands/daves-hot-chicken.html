CSW Analysis

Dave's is the fastest-growing large chain in America and the single most important development story in chicken real estate. Sales grew 51% and units 52.3% in 2025, Roark bought control at a $1B valuation, and the brand opened eight restaurants in one day in 2026. Franchisee-reported AUVs above $3M with 18–20% restaurant-level EBITDA margins explain why sophisticated multi-brand operators are stacking Dave's on top of existing portfolios — the payback math beats almost anything else they can buy. The risk is the standard hot-chicken risk: the concept is culturally hot right now, and roughly 1,000 units in development means the system will be selling into far less proven trade areas within 24 months than the ones that produced those AUVs.

Real estate profile

Cap rate
Trades tight for a young franchisee credit; brand-record cap rates have been set on new-construction drive-thru prototypes2

Dave's began as a 1,200–1,800 SF inline concept and has moved decisively to freestanding drive-thru prototypes on 15-year absolute-NNN leases with 10% increases every five years. Franchisee-reported AUVs above $3M against a small box are the reason net-lease buyers accept franchisee credit here at pricing normally reserved for corporate tenants.

Dave's Hot Chicken in the news

2026-04Development

Dave's Hot Chicken opens eight restaurants in a single day1

What it means for the Chicken Wars: With 150+ openings planned for 2026 and roughly 1,000 units in development, Dave's is consuming drive-thru pad sites faster than any brand in the category. It is now the marginal bidder on a large share of American QSR real estate — and the reason second-generation chicken boxes are clearing quickly.

2025-06M&A

Roark Capital acquires a majority stake in Dave's Hot Chicken at a $1B valuation3

What it means for the Chicken Wars: A 245-unit brand valued at $1B — roughly $4M per restaurant, against domestic systemwide sales of $617M. Roark underwrote the development pipeline, not the current cash flow. Everything Dave's has done since (150+ openings in 2026, eight in a single day, ~1,000 in development) is that thesis being executed.
