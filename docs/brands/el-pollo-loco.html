CSW Analysis

El Pollo Loco is the category's counter-programming: flame-grilled, Mexican-inspired, and largely insulated from the fried-chicken arms race. It is also the smallest growth story here — nine openings in a year against a 500-unit base, and flat full-year comps of +0.1%. The Q4 acceleration to +2.1% and a 17.8% restaurant contribution margin say the operating model is healthy; the constraint is geographic concentration in nine states and a build-cost profile that makes franchise development slower than a fryer-only competitor's.
