CSW Analysis

Houston TX Hot Chicken is the clearest example of the second wave chasing Dave's. The brand is going after the same hot-chicken consumer with a smaller box and a lower entry cost, and it is signing multi-unit commitments in markets — Illinois, Arkansas, New York — where Dave's has not yet saturated. The European deal with PizzaExpress is unusual and worth watching: partnering with an established operator platform is a faster route into the UK than building a franchise network from scratch.
