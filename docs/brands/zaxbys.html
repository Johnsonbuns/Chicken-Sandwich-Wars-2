CSW Analysis

Zaxby's spent the last several years fixing operations rather than chasing unit count, and the payoff is a $2.71M AUV that sits second only to Chick-fil-A and Cane's among franchised chicken chains. Crossing 1,000 units and then walking into the Bronx and the Upper East Side is a genuine test: Zaxby's has never had to make a 2,800 SF, seat-constrained, no-drive-thru box work at Southeast economics. If it does, the brand's franchise recruiting pitch changes completely, because the addressable market stops being the Southeast and starts being every dense metro where Cane's and Wingstop already print volume.

Zaxby's in the news

2026-07Development

Zaxby's opens its first New York City restaurant in the Bronx6

What it means for the Chicken Wars: A Southeast chicken brand with a 3,500 SF suburban dine-in DNA is now operating in the Bronx, with a 2,800 SF, 16-seat Upper East Side unit behind it. If the small-format Northeast box clears anywhere near the $2.71M system AUV, Zaxby's franchise recruiting stops being a regional pitch.

2026-02Brands

Zaxby's surpasses 1,000 locations1

What it means for the Chicken Wars: From 969 units at the end of 2024 to more than 1,000, with a projected $2.8B in system sales and a $2.71M AUV — the third-highest unit volume in the category behind Chick-fil-A and Cane's. Zaxby's has quietly become the strongest franchised chicken investment outside the top two.
