CSW Analysis

Golden Chick is the best case study in this database for how a regional brand actually scales: not through a national franchise-sales push, but by finding two or three operator families and letting them compound. SXThree is on a path to 100 units by 2030; the Korpal brothers converted a second-generation Subway family operation into a 13-unit Golden Chick development agreement across Houston and Beaumont. Every incremental unit is being bought by someone who already owns units. For a brokerage or capital provider, that concentration is the opportunity — there are perhaps a dozen phone calls that control the brand's entire Texas development pipeline.

2026-01Franchising

Korpal Group signs a 13-unit Golden Chick development agreement for Houston and Beaumont3

What it means for the Chicken Wars: Ten Houston units over four years and three in Beaumont over ten. Second-generation Subway operators redeploying family capital into a higher-AUV chicken concept is the single most common pattern in Texas chicken development right now — and it means ten Houston sites need to be found, entitled and built by an operator who does not yet control them.
