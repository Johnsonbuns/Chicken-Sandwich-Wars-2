CSW Analysis

Huey Magoo's roughly doubled from 43 to 85 units in under three years and cut its drive-thru build cost by about 40%. That second number is the real story. Emerging chicken brands do not die from lack of demand — they die when a $2.2M build cost meets a $1.6M AUV. Getting the prototype cheaper is what turns a Florida tender brand into a franchisable national concept.
