CSW Analysis

Korean fried chicken is the most under-built segment of the American chicken market relative to consumer interest. Bonchon's move into airports is a rational read of its own economics: a high-ticket, dine-in-leaning, sauce-forward concept monetizes captive traffic better than it monetizes suburban drive-thru traffic.
