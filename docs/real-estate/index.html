Lease intelligence

Chick-fil-A. 20-year corporate absolute-NNN ground lease is standard; the record-setting Hixson TN deal was a shorter 15-year term with 10% increases every five years and ten 5-year options.6

Raising Cane's. Typically a 20-year absolute-NNN ground lease with 10% rent increases every five years, corporate guaranteed — landlord has no roof, structure, parking or management obligation.3

Popeyes. 15–20 year initial terms with 7.5%–10% rent bumps every five years; historical pricing band roughly $1.2M–$4.1M per asset.4

Dave's Hot Chicken. New-construction drive-thru prototypes on 15-year absolute-NNN leases with 10% increases every five years.7

Credit spread. The corporate-to-franchisee QSR spread stood at 100 basis points in Q2 2026 (5.85% vs 6.85%). Short-term, non-rated restaurant tenants can trade 200–300 bps wider than premium ground leases.2

Closures & vacancy supply

Second-generation chicken inventory currently being created:

KFC
312 U.S. closures in the twelve months to July 2026 (-7.64% of the footprint); 161 closures in calendar 2025 against 15 openings.10

Popeyes
Roughly 33 Sailormen locations went dark through lease rejection in the 2026 Chapter 11 across Florida and Georgia.11

Hooters
Roughly 30 restaurants closed effective June 4, 2025 across FL, GA, MI, NC, SC, TN and TX — large-format casual-dining pads, 4,500–6,500 SF.12

Church's Texas Chicken
12 units ceased operations in 2025 (after 20 in 2024 and nine the year prior), plus one franchise termination and one corporate closure.13

Jollibee
More than 200 stores closed group-wide in the first half of 2026 with the 2026 opening target cut to 1,000–1,100.14

Market conditions

Single-tenant property supply jumped 12.5% quarter-over-quarter in Q2 2026 to nearly 5,800 available properties, with retail listings up 16.2%.1

Boulder Group's Q1 2026 Net Lease Tenant Profiles report covered 87 tenants with asking cap rates as of March 2026, ranging from 4.30% for 15-year McDonald's ground leases to 9.00%+ for short-term Walgreens.2

Four Corners Property Trust acquired 33 properties for $85.5M in the first half of 2026 (10 for $26.8M in Q1, 23 for $57.2M in Q2).15
