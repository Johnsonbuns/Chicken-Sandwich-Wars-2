Market profile · Florida

Tampa & North Florida

A 50-restaurant Popeyes platform was just recapitalized here at roughly $54,000 per store. That basis will define competitive behavior in these trade areas for years.

Tracked activity

  • Pulse Restaurant Group — established by Sailormen CEO David Damato — acquired 50 Popeyes restaurants across Tampa, Tallahassee, Pensacola and Jacksonville for $2.69M1
  • SBH Foods PLK LLC acquired five Savannah-area restaurants for $650,0001
  • Hooters closures in June 2025 removed large-format casual dining pads across Florida2

What CSW is watching here

  • Whether a debt-free 50-unit operator becomes an acquirer of additional Florida chicken assets
  • Landlord outcomes on Sailormen leases that were assumed versus rejected
  • Second-generation demand for the vacated Hooters pads
