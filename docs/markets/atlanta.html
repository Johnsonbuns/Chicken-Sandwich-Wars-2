Market profile · Georgia

Atlanta, GA

More chicken brand headquarters sit in metro Atlanta than anywhere else in the country — including the largest and one of the smallest by unit volume.

Tracked activity

  • Chick-fil-A is headquartered in Atlanta and closed 2025 with $23.9B in systemwide sales on a $9.16M average unit volume1
  • Church's Texas Chicken is headquartered in Atlanta with 885 U.S. stores and a $1.138M AUV2
  • Zaxby's is headquartered in Athens, GA and surpassed 1,000 locations in February 20263
  • Chicken Salad Chick is headquartered in Atlanta and passed 300 locations in 20254

What CSW is watching here

  • Executive and franchise-development talent movement among four headquartered brands
  • Whether Church's Atlanta-based turnaround holds a second consecutive year of unit growth
