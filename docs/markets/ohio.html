Market profile · Ohio

Ohio (Columbus / Cincinnati)

The clearest test market for Southeast brand portability — two brands with no Ohio history are committing to meaningful unit counts here at the same time.

Tracked activity

  • Eyas Capital, now Bojangles' largest franchisee after acquiring 120-unit BOJ of WNC, agreed to grow Bojangles in Cincinnati and Columbus to more than 40 locations1
  • Slim Chickens named Ohio among its priority national expansion markets2
  • Zaxby's is expanding across the Midwest and Northeast with 60+ openings targeted in 20263

What CSW is watching here

  • Whether out-of-region Bojangles units clear the $2.35M system AUV
  • Breakfast daypart performance for a Carolina brand in Midwest commuter patterns
  • Site competition among three Southeast brands entering the same metros simultaneously
