Why the operator layer matters

Case study

120 restaurants changed hands. The brand's unit count did not move.

Eyas Capital acquired BOJ of WNC, Bojangles' largest franchise group, in a six-state portfolio deal1. Nothing in brand-level data would show you that — or the 40+ Ohio units the new owner immediately committed to.

Case study

A 136-unit operator disappeared in eleven months.

Sailormen filed Chapter 11 in January 2026 with $233.5M of fiscal 2025 revenue2, weeks after its franchisor disclosed average restaurant profit of roughly $235,0003. The warning was in operator economics, not brand sales.

Case study

One operator controls a quarter of a national system.

KBP Brands runs 831 KFC restaurants4 while the brand closes 300+ U.S. locations a year5 — making it simultaneously the biggest buyer and biggest potential seller of KFC assets in America.
