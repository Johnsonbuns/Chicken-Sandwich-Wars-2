CSW Analysis

A textbook second-generation conversion: a family that built a 22-unit Subway business redeploying into a higher-AUV chicken concept with a 13-unit commitment across Houston and Beaumont. Ten Houston sites over four years is a real, dated pipeline — and it is the kind of commitment that gets made before the sites are secured.
