CSW Analysis

Far West is the largest concentrated Wingstop position on the West Coast and it is institutionally capitalized, which matters more in 2026 than it did in 2024: Wingstop domestic comps fell 7.5% in Q2 2026 with pressure concentrated in urban trade areas — precisely where a California-heavy portfolio sits. A private-equity-backed operator with a 51-unit development commitment into a negative-comp environment is the situation most likely to produce either aggressive site take-downs or a pipeline renegotiation.
