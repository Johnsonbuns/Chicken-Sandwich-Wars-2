CSW Analysis

KBP is the single most important counterparty in U.S. KFC real estate. With 831 KFC units it controls roughly a quarter of the entire U.S. KFC system at a moment when the brand is closing more than 300 restaurants a year — which means KBP is simultaneously the biggest buyer of KFC assets and the biggest source of KFC dispositions. Any strategy involving KFC boxes, second-generation conversions or franchise transfers runs through this operator.
