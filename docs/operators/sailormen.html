CSW Analysis

Sailormen is the case study every chicken lender and landlord should be reading. A 136-unit operator generating $233.5M of revenue filed Chapter 11 within twelve months of Popeyes franchisee restaurant-level profit falling to roughly $235,000 per store. The portfolio cleared at $16.55M for 97 restaurants — about $171,000 per operating restaurant for the business — and 33 locations went dark through lease rejection. Those dark boxes are now the most interesting second-generation chicken inventory in Florida and Georgia.
